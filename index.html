The Alentejo does not hurry. Neither will you.

Malhão Pardo is a working estate turned boutique resort — a handful of rooms, one long table, a pool that holds the evening light. Days begin with bread still warm and end under more stars than you remember existing.

The estate

Whitewash, cork
and quiet geometry.

Low white volumes sit lightly on the land, the way this region has always built — thick walls against the heat, blue shadow in the doorways, a pine holding the sky. Inside: linen, clay, timber. Nothing that shouts.

Twelve rooms across the monte. Never more.

The pool

Where the evening
comes to swim.

Warm stone underfoot, a long still line of water, umbrellas leaning into the last sun. Order a glass of something cold from the honesty bar and stay until the swifts come out.

When is the best time to come? +
Every season argues its case. Spring for wildflowers, June for the cork harvest, high summer for pool days and warm nights, autumn for the light, winter for fires and near-total silence. The estate stays open all year.
Can we book the whole estate? +
Yes — for gatherings, weddings and retreats the twelve rooms and the Casa do Monte take up to 30 guests. Write to us and we'll plan it properly.
Are children and dogs welcome? +
Children, always. Dogs, by arrangement in the Casa do Monte — the estate has sheep for neighbours, so we plan it together.
How does dinner work? +
One seating at 20:00, one menu from what the garden and market gave us that day. Tell us about allergies when you book; the kitchen takes them seriously.
